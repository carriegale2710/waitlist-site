Transparency you can trust

Our vetting process combines occupational therapy expertise with real-world disabled user feedback to ensure every product meets genuine accessibility needs.

01

Expert OT Review

Every product is evaluated by certified Occupational Therapists and reviewed by hundreds of real users with disabilities.

02

Clinical Criteria Content

Products are assessed against established OT criteria, lab tests, and usability data based on specific disability categories.

03

Biomechanical Compatibility

We assess all physical factors including grip strength, reach range, and fine motor ability requirements.
